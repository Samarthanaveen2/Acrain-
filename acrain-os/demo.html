Acrain OS

A working phone operating system, running in this page. Nothing here is a mockup: every app does what it says, and they talk to each other.

  • UnlockClick the lock screen. The wallpaper is computed live on your graphics chip and follows your pointer.
  • BeatsPress Play, then paint steps on the grid. Go home and the music keeps running.
  • TerminalType help. Try beats play, accent ember or matrix.
  • GravityDrag through 2,400 particles. Use several fingers on a touch screen.
  • HomeClick the bar at the bottom of the phone, or press Esc.
