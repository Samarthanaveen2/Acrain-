By 2030, the question isn't whether AI is transformative. It's who controls it, and whether it stays under control at all. The story can branch hard from here, and I don't know which way it goes.

What I do know is what I want to be true of me by then: that I understood this early, built things that were useful while building was still a human advantage, and kept enough options open to move fast when the ground shifted.

:::expand[My checklist for the next 12 months]
- Re-read this scenario every three months and update the numbers
- Ship one real project with AI agents doing most of the work
- Follow AI policy news weekly, not just product launches
- Keep one skill in the physical world, where robots are still behind
:::
