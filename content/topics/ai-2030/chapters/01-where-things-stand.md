The AI companies are now among the biggest businesses in the world. Anthropic reportedly passed $47 billion in annualized revenue in May and raised money at a $965 billion valuation, edging past OpenAI's $852 billion from March.[^arr] Reports disagree on OpenAI's exact revenue, somewhere between $25 and $40 billion a year.[^techloy]

The money going into hardware is even bigger. The largest cloud companies plan to spend more than $700 billion on data centers this year, most of it for AI.[^capex]

The models are changing what "using AI" means. On METR's benchmark, the best agents now finish software tasks that take a skilled human around 14 hours, and that length has been doubling every few months.[^metr] Agents don't just answer questions anymore; they go off and do work.

:::expand[The AI R&D progress multiplier: what the lines on the chart mean]
The chart at the top of the dashboard shows how much faster AI research moves with AI help than without it. A multiplier of 1.5 means a lab makes a year and a half of progress in one calendar year.

This is the number that matters most. If AI speeds up AI research, every other line on the dashboard bends upward with it. The values here are my estimates, in the spirit of AI 2027's version of the same chart.
:::

The public hasn't made up its mind. One poll this spring found 26% of voters positive about AI and 46% negative;[^nbc] another in July found 56% favorable.[^harrisx] The wording of the question seems to decide the answer.
