GTA VI launches on November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with no PC version announced.[^date] Within hours, people are dumping it from modified consoles and pointing AnyPS5 at it.

My guess: it doesn't run playably that week. A game this size leans on PlayStation-specific systems that a young project hasn't touched yet, and skeptics note that PS3 and PS4 compatibility each took years.[^skeptic] What *does* happen is smaller games. By the end of the month, a handful more PS5 titles run natively on PC.

The attention matters more than the result. Millions of people who never cared about emulation now know the word "AnyPS5", and so do Sony's lawyers.

:::expand[What I'm watching this month]
- Whether AnyPS5 publishes a release at all (the developer says not until a full game launches through it)
- Whether Sony sends a DMCA notice to the repo
- ArtCraft's beta date. The developer promised near-parity in a month.
:::
