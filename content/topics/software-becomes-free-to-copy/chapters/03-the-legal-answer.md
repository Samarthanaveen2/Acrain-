The companies respond the way Nintendo did with Yuzu in 2024: lawyers first. I expect takedown notices against at least one ArtCraft app over its look and name, and pressure on AnyPS5's hosting.

It doesn't work the way they hope. Each takedown teaches the next project what to avoid. Clones rename themselves, change their icons, and keep shipping. The code is MIT-licensed and already forked a hundred times, so removing one repo removes nothing.

The more important change is quieter. Developers who saw ArtCraft start pointing agents at the apps *they* pay for: CAD tools, audio software, project trackers. Most of these attempts are bad. A few are good enough.

:::expand[The Yuzu precedent]
Nintendo sued the Yuzu Switch emulator in 2024, and the developers shut down and paid a $2.4M settlement. The emulator's code survived in forks anyway. The lesson the industry took: you can stop a *team*, but you can't un-publish code.
:::
